YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

SmartPick Hub aims to collect only the information needed to provide its current website features. This policy explains the information handled by the account and newsletter features currently present in the project. Some shopping, recommendation, analytics, and personalisation features described elsewhere are planned and are not yet active.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: If you create an account, Supabase Auth processes your email address, password credential, authentication sessions, verification status, and account timestamps. SmartPick Hub does not receive or store your plain-text password.
  • Display name: If you edit your profile, the display name is stored in your Supabase Auth user metadata and used to personalise account navigation.
  • Newsletter email: If you subscribe, your email address and subscription status are processed by the newsletter backend. A confirmation link is required before the subscription is activated. Token values are stored as hashes in the database.
  • Technical information: Hosting, security, and service providers may process basic connection and diagnostic information as part of operating their services. The exact logs and retention periods depend on the final hosting configuration.

2. How information is used

  • To create and secure accounts, sign users in, and support password recovery.
  • To display the account name selected by the user.
  • To send newsletter messages only after email confirmation and to process unsubscribe requests.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the website and its services.

3. Service providers

The current project integrates Supabase for authentication and newsletter database operations, and Resend for newsletter email delivery. The website may also be hosted by a separate hosting provider. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy policies. Confirm the final provider configuration and data-region settings before launch.

4. Newsletter choices

Newsletter signup is a separate, optional action. You must confirm your email address before the subscription becomes active. You can use the unsubscribe link included with newsletter messages. Ignoring a confirmation email leaves the subscription unconfirmed.

5. Sharing and advertising

SmartPick Hub does not sell personal information. If a page contains an affiliate link, the retailer may receive information when you choose to visit or purchase from that retailer. Read our Affiliate Disclosure. An optional outbound-click tracking foundation is present in the code, but it is not active until the backend is deployed, configured, and its database migration is applied. If enabled, it records only the product ID, whether the destination is an affiliate or direct retailer link, and the click timestamp. It does not intentionally collect visitor IP addresses, user-agent strings, account IDs, or referrers. This records link clicks only; it does not identify purchases or commissions.

6. Security and retention

The project is designed to keep server-side credentials out of browser code and restrict private database records through access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the relevant account or subscription and to meet applicable operational or legal requirements. Exact deletion and retention procedures must be confirmed before production launch.

7. Your choices and requests

You can manage your account password through the account recovery flow and unsubscribe from newsletter emails. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact the SmartPick Hub team using the contact method published on the live website. A public contact address has not yet been configured in this development version.

8. Children

SmartPick Hub is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not legally permitted to use the service in their location. A parent or guardian may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features and providers change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact

A dedicated privacy contact email has not yet been configured. Add and verify a business contact address before opening the website to the public.
